When you burn an eye with the laser, what should it feel like?

This sets the tone for every "power" interaction (burn/eat/smear/stamp) across all three modes

A

Satisfying pop & dissolve

Eye briefly glows red at the pupil, a thin laser line draws from cursor to target, then the eye shrinks and dissolves into a puff of ash/particles. Cathartic, cartoonish, quick (~400ms). Matches the flat-illustration warmth of your reference image — more "stress toy" than "violence."

B

Char & crack, then crumble

Laser scorches the eye shape black/brown from the pupil outward like burning paper, edges crack, then it crumbles into falling ash flakes that fade before hitting the ground. Slower (~800ms), more visceral and "protest effigy burning" in tone.

C

Comic-book zap

Bright cartoon "ZAP!" burst (like a comic sound-effect graphic) flashes over the eye, eye flattens into a squashed splat shape then fades. Playful, meme-able, leans into shareability over catharsis.
