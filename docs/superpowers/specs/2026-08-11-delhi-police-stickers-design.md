# Delhi Police Constable Sticker Sheet Design Spec

## Overview
A sticker sheet featuring 12 caricatured Delhi Police male constables from a top-down drone perspective.

## Design Goals
- **Subject:** 12 male Delhi Police constables.
- **Uniform:** Standard khaki (beige) uniform, beige beret with red badge, black belts, tactical gear, walkie-talkies.
- **Accessories:** Lathis (traditional bamboo sticks) carried or held by the constables.
- **Perspective:** Extreme top-down elevated "drone-shot" view.
- **Art Style:** Bold marker style with thick black outlines, vibrant marker colors, and a color halftone pattern for texture and shading.
- **Format:** Sticker sheet with a dynamic, slightly scattered layout. Each sticker has a thick white die-cut border.

## Technical Requirements
- **Output:** A single high-resolution image.
- **Prompt Components:** 
  - Subject: "12 caricature Delhi Police male constables in khaki uniforms with caps, gear, and lathis"
  - Angle: "top-down elevated drone-shot, viewing directly from above"
  - Style: "bold marker style, thick black outlines, color halftone, vibrant color marker style"
  - Packaging: "sticker sheet, dynamic scattered layout, thick white border around each sticker"

## Success Criteria
- [ ] 12 distinct constables are visible.
- [ ] Perspective is clearly top-down.
- [ ] Uniforms accurately represent Delhi Police (khaki, berets).
- [ ] Art style matches the "bold marker + halftone" request.
- [ ] White borders and sticker sheet layout are clear.
