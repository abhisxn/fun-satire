# RAF Sticker Sheets Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Generate 5 high-diversity sticker sheets for the Indian Rapid Action Force (RAF) featuring **full-body caricatures** from elevated drone perspectives on a dark grey background.

**Architecture:** Use the `generate_image` tool with precise prompts specifying a 3x4 grid, head-to-toe figures, and unique drone angles for each sheet.

**Tech Stack:** Kilo `generate_image` tool.

---

### Task 1: Generate RAF Sticker Sheet 1 (60° Angle)

**Files:**
- Create: `public/assets/stickers/raf-stickers-1.png`

- [ ] **Step 1: Invoke image generation for Sheet 1**

Use the following prompt:
"A high-quality sticker sheet containing **exactly 12 unique and diverse full-body** caricatured Indian Rapid Action Force (RAF) male personnel in blue camouflage uniforms and blue helmets, arranged in a **strict 3x4 grid**. Every character is a different individual with unique faces and gear, shown from **head to toe including shoes**. **Dark grey background (#1a1a1a).** Poses include pointing and holding lathis. Expressions are varied **intense angry disgust**. Perspective: **60-degree elevated drone-shot angle** looking down at the full figures. Style: **Bold caricature illustration**, thick black outlines, vibrant marker colors, color halftone shading. Each sticker has a thick white die-cut border. **Strictly 12 stickers total, no overlap.**"

Path: `public/assets/stickers/raf-stickers-1`
