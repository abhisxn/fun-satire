# Delhi Police Constable Sticker Sheet Design Spec

## Overview
A sticker sheet featuring 12 caricatured Delhi Police male constables from a top-down drone perspective.

## Design Goals
- **Subject:** 12 **highly unique and individual** Delhi Police male constables. Each character must have a distinct facial structure, age (ranging from young to middle-aged), and mustache style.
- **Diversity:** Include characters with different features: some with spectacles, some with thicker builds, some leaner. Poses must vary significantly: one talking on a walkie-talkie, one pointing aggressively, one with arms crossed, one holding a lathi over the shoulder, one looking at a clipboard, one blowing a whistle.
- **Expressions:** All show **varied** intense expressions of angry disgust—scowling, yelling, sneering, frowning—no two faces should look like clones.
- **Uniform & Gear:** Standard khaki uniform and **beige berets** on all. Gear configurations must be randomized across the grid.
- **Perspective:** Extremely steep elevated drone-shot view (approx 80° angle) showing faces and shoulders.
- **Art Style:** Bold marker style with thick black outlines, vibrant marker colors, and a color halftone pattern for texture and shading.
- **Format:** Sticker sheet with a **strict uniform grid layout (3x4)**. Each sticker is distinct with **no overlap** and a thick white die-cut border. The background of the sheet should be a **solid dark grey**.

## Technical Requirements
- **Output:** A single high-resolution image.
- **Prompt Components:** 
  - Subject: "12 **diverse and unique** caricature Delhi Police male constables. Each sticker is a **completely different character design** with unique faces, ages, and gear. One has spectacles, one yells into a walkie-talkie, one points a finger, one holds a lathi, one has arms crossed. All have unique expressions of **intense angry disgust**."
  - Angle: "extremely elevated drone-shot, steep overhead angle showing faces from above"
  - Style: "bold marker style, thick black outlines, color halftone, vibrant color marker style"
  - Packaging: "sticker sheet, strict 3x4 grid layout, no overlap, thick white border around each sticker, **solid dark grey background**"

## Success Criteria
- [ ] **Exactly 12 unique and distinct** constables are visible (zero clones).
- [ ] **High variation in poses, gear, and facial features** (e.g., glasses, different mustache types).
- [ ] Characters are arranged in a clean, uniform **3x4 grid** with no overlaps.
- [ ] Expressions are clearly visible, intense, and **varied**.
- [ ] Perspective is extremely elevated but shows faces.
- [ ] Uniforms accurately represent Delhi Police (khaki, beige berets).
- [ ] Art style matches the "bold marker + halftone" request.
- [ ] White borders are clear and separate against the **dark grey background**.
